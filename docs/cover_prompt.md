# 封面图像提示词

## 中文版

一座现代化火力发电厂的汽轮机厂房内部透视，巨大的汽轮发电机组位于画面中央，不锈钢管道纵横交错连接着锅炉与凝汽器，蒸汽从法兰接口处微微逸出。背景是高耸的锅炉钢结构框架，透过厂房顶部的天窗可以看到蓝天白云。整体采用赛博朋克与工业写实相融合的艺术风格，冷色调的钢铁灰蓝与暖色调的蒸汽橙金形成对比。使用低角度仰视构图，强调设备的宏伟与工业力量感，汽轮机组占据画面三分之二。顶部天窗投下的丁达尔光束穿过蒸汽弥漫的空气，形成戏剧性的体积光效果，设备表面反射出冷冽的金属高光。画面中点缀热力学参数的半透明技术标注（温度、压力、焓值），管道上可见精密的压力表与阀门细节，地面有规律地铺设着防滑钢板格栅。

## English version

A cross-section interior view of a modern thermal power plant turbine hall. A massive turbo-generator unit sits at the center of the frame, with stainless steel pipes crisscrossing to connect the boiler and condenser, wisps of steam gently escaping from flange joints. In the background rises the towering steel framework of the boiler, with blue sky and white clouds visible through skylights in the roof. Art style: a fusion of cyberpunk and industrial realism, contrasting cool steel-blue-gray tones with warm steam-orange-gold highlights. Camera: low-angle upward shot emphasizing the grandeur of the equipment and the sense of industrial power, with the turbine unit occupying two-thirds of the frame. Lighting: Tyndall beams streaming through the skylights pierce the steam-filled air, creating dramatic volumetric lighting effects, with cold metallic highlights reflecting off equipment surfaces. Details: semi-transparent technical annotations showing thermodynamic parameters (temperature, pressure, enthalpy) overlaid subtly, precision pressure gauges and valves visible on the pipes, anti-slip steel grating laid in orderly patterns on the floor.

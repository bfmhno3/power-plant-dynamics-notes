# Logo 提示词

## 中文版

一枚极简主义风格的圆形徽标设计，主体是一片高度几何化的汽轮机叶片剖面，叶片轮廓由三条流畅的弧线勾勒而成，内部填充渐变的蓝橙双色（从冷蓝过渡到暖橙，象征热能到机械能的转换）。叶片后方环绕着一圈抽象的蒸汽涡旋纹样，用细线条表现。整体采用扁平化矢量设计风格，线条干净利落，无多余装饰。正视图，对称构图，叶片居中。背景为纯白或深藏青色，叶片上的渐变色在光线下呈现微妙的金属质感。徽标外圈是一条极细的环形轮廓线，整体尺寸适合用于书籍封面角标或章节页眉。

## English version

A minimalist circular emblem design. The main element is a highly geometric cross-section of a steam turbine blade, its silhouette defined by three smooth flowing arcs, filled with a gradient of blue to orange (transitioning from cool blue to warm orange, symbolizing the conversion of thermal energy to mechanical energy). Behind the blade, an abstract steam vortex pattern is rendered in fine thin lines. Overall flat vector design style with clean, sharp lines and no unnecessary decoration. Front view, symmetrical composition, blade centered. Background is pure white or deep navy blue, with the gradient on the blade showing subtle metallic sheen under light. The emblem is encircled by an ultra-thin ring outline, sized appropriately for use as a book cover corner mark or chapter header.
